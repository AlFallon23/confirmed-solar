What you get for every address

Lists are delivered as Excel or CSV, cut to your counties or your criteria.

On every address

Street addressThe property address.
City and ZIPWhat goes on an envelope.
County and stateFor filtering to your service area.
Confirmation gradeEach record is labelled with how it was confirmed.

On most addresses

Install month and yearExact, not estimated. Panel age follows from it, so you can pull systems that are ten years old or more.
System size in kWExact nameplate capacity.
Array footprint and edge lengthDerived from system size and given as ranges. The edge length helps size a job before you drive out.

Where available

Array size rankingLarger or smaller than typical. A ranking for prioritising calls, not a measurement.
Roof complexityNumber of roof planes. Two is simple, eight or more means a fiddlier job.
Panel typeMonocrystalline or polycrystalline, with a confidence tier: firm, likely or uncertain.
Coordinates and parcel IDTo drop a pin on the roof or reconcile against county records.

Not available

  • Roof age. It does not exist in any public source, and I will not pass off home age as roof age.
  • Owner names, phone numbers and email addresses. The standard lists are property information only.

Good to know before you buy

  • It is a list of confirmed homes, not every solar home in a county. The list keeps growing.
  • Systems installed in the last few years may not be on it yet.
  • I do not flag whether a home is owned or leased.
  • Install date and size come with most addresses, not all. Ask for a count in your counties.
