About

I'm Alex Fallon. I graduated from Gettysburg College in 2026, and I built Confirmed Solar myself.

I started it because there was no way to know which houses had solar short of driving the streets. Companies that work on existing systems had no list to call from, so I made one and kept going until it covered tens of thousands of homes.

It is one person doing the work. That means when you call or email, you reach me, and when you ask for a county I don't have yet, I can run it.

How I work

  • Every address on the list has been confirmed to have panels. I don't sell guesses.
  • If I don't know something, I say I'll check and come back.
  • Samples are free so you can verify before you buy anything.
