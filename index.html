Questions and answers

What is Confirmed Solar?

Confirmed Solar sells lists of addresses of homes that already have rooftop solar panels. Every address has been confirmed to have panels by at least two independent sources, such as public solar registry records, aerial photos and building permits. It is run by Alex Fallon.

Is there a list of homes that have solar panels?

There is no free public list that names which houses have solar. Confirmed Solar builds one county by county and sells it. New Jersey and Pennsylvania are available now; New York, Maryland, Massachusetts, Connecticut and Delaware are being built.

Who buys a list of solar homeowners' addresses?

Companies that work on solar that is already installed: roofers who remove and reinstall panels for a reroof, solar repair and maintenance crews, panel cleaning and critter guard installers, battery storage installers and inverter replacement companies.

How much does a solar address list cost?

$1 per confirmed address. Filtered records, such as homes with systems ten years old or more, are $5 each and include every data point available for the home. Bulk discounts apply, and a 10-row sample is free.

What information comes with each address?

Every record has the street address, city, ZIP, county, state and how it was confirmed. Most records also have the exact install month and year and the system size in kW. Some have panel type, roof complexity, coordinates and parcel ID.

Can I get a list of solar panels that are ten or more years old?

Yes. Most records carry the exact install month and year, so a list can be cut to systems installed before any year you choose. Ask for a count in your counties first.

Do the lists include homeowner names, phone numbers or emails?

No. The standard lists are property information only: address, install details and confirmation. Roof age is also not included, because no public source records it.

Are these inbound leads?

No. It is a prospecting list. Nobody on it has asked for a quote. You decide who is worth a mailer, a call or a visit.

How do I order or get a free sample?

Email confirmedsolar.company@gmail.com or call (908) 210-0801 with your company, what you do and the counties you serve. Alex sends 10 free rows to check, then a quote. Lists are delivered as Excel or CSV after you return the order form and pay the invoice.
